# References

The publications behind every dataset of spdnet-datasets. When you report results
obtained on a dataset, cite its original publication (and the data record when
one is listed); each dataset page repeats its entry in a *How to cite* block.

```{note}
Every DOI below was resolved on [doi.org](https://doi.org) and its title and
authors checked against the Crossref or DataCite record. Entries without a DOI
(technical reports, competitions, a dataset without publication) link to the
source page instead.
```
